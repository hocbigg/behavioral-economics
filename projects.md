## Run a Behavioral Experiment

- [How to Design (GREAT) Experiments: A Comprehensive Guide (Center for Advanced Hindsight)](https://advanced-hindsight.com/blog/how-to-design-great-experiments-comprehensive-guide) - A step-by-step walkthrough from Dan Ariely's research center covering hypothesis formation, experimental design, sample size, and analysis, written for practitioners.
- [Checklist for Launching a Randomized Evaluation in the United States (J-PAL North America)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/checklist-launching-randomized-evaluation-united-states) - A practical checklist for structuring, launching, and managing a randomized evaluation, covering administrative steps, partner relationships, and data logistics.
- [Introduction to Randomized Evaluations (J-PAL)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/introduction-randomized-evaluations) - A non-technical overview of when and how to use randomized evaluations, with examples from real studies across multiple sectors.

## Write a Behavioral Policy Brief

- [A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab)](https://www.capolicylab.org) - A 6-page guide specifically focused on writing policy briefs that translate academic research into actionable recommendations for government.
- [How to Write a Policy Brief (IDRC / BetterEvaluation)](https://www.betterevaluation.org) - A step-by-step outline from the International Development Research Centre covering purpose, structure, and length for effective policy briefs.
- [Writing Briefs (CDC POLARIS)](https://www.cdc.gov) - The CDC's framework for four types of briefing documents, useful for understanding how to present evidence to decision-makers at different stages of the policy process.

## Design a Nudge or Choice Architecture Intervention

- [Tools and Ethics for Applied Behavioural Insights: The BASIC Toolkit (OECD)](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2019/06/tools-and-ethics-for-applied-behavioural-insights-the-basic-toolkit_bbbaaa7a/9ea76a8f-en.pdf) - A comprehensive, freely downloadable toolkit walking through five sequential stages (Behaviour, Analysis, Strategies, Intervention, Change) for designing behaviorally informed policy interventions.
- [The EAST Framework (Behavioural Insights Team)](https://www.bi.team/publications/east-four-simple-ways-to-apply-behavioural-insights) - BIT's practical framework for designing behavioral interventions, distilling a decade of field experience into four principles: Easy, Attractive, Social, and Timely. Includes worked examples.

## Write a Behavioral Case Study Analysis

- [The Behavioral Economics Guide (Alain Samson, ed.)](https://www.behavioraleconomics.com/be-guide) - An annually updated reference containing case studies applying behavioral economics frameworks to real-world decisions. The case study sections provide models for your own analysis.
- [Writing a Case Analysis Paper (USC Writing Center)](https://libguides.usc.edu/writingguide/assignments/caseanalysis) - A university guide covering structure, tone, and evidence standards for case analysis writing, applicable to behavioral economics cases.

## Conduct a Behavioral Diagnosis

- [Tools and Ethics for Applied Behavioural Insights: The BASIC Toolkit (OECD)](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2019/06/tools-and-ethics-for-applied-behavioural-insights-the-basic-toolkit_bbbaaa7a/9ea76a8f-en.pdf) - The Behaviour and Analysis stages of this framework provide a structured methodology for identifying behavioral barriers and drivers in a policy or product context.
