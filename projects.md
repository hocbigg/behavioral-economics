## Map your own behavioral decision pathways

- [The 3B Framework & Behavioral Design Guide (Irrational Labs)](https://irrationallabs.com/download-behavioral-design-guide/) - A step-by-step practitioner methodology for isolating target behaviors, mapping choice environments, and diagnosing psychological barriers.
- [Behavioral Design: A New Approach to Development Policy (Datta & Mullainathan / ideas42)](https://doi.org/10.1111/roiw.12093) - A foundational methodological guide detailing ideas42's behavioral mapping process to locate decision bottlenecks and contextual stress points.

## Conduct your own sludge audits

- [The NSW Government Sludge Audit Method Guide (NSW Behavioural Insights Unit)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/behavioural-insights-unit/sludge-toolkit) - The premier open public-sector toolkit for auditing, measuring, and eliminating administrative burdens, form complexities, and cognitive frictions.
- [Fixing Frictions: 'Sludge Audits' Around the World (OECD)](https://doi.org/10.1787/5e9bb35c-en) - A comprehensive policy paper providing nine good practice principles and ten real-world case studies for conducting institutional sludge audits.

## Design your own nudge interventions

- [The EAST Framework: Four Simple Ways to Apply Behavioural Insights (The Behavioural Insights Team)](https://www.bi.team/publications/east-four-simple-ways-to-apply-behavioural-insights/) - The benchmark field manual providing actionable heuristics and case studies to make desired behaviors Easy, Attractive, Social, and Timely.
- [Tools and Ethics for Applied Behavioural Insights: The BASIC Toolkit (OECD)](https://doi.org/10.1787/9ea76a8f-en) - An operational 5-stage policy framework covering behavioral definition, qualitative analysis, strategy selection, ethical evaluation, and change management.

## Register your own pre-analysis plans

- [Pre-Analysis Plans: A Guide for Researchers (J-PAL)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/pre-analysis-plans) - A detailed guide explaining how to specify hypotheses, econometric models, primary outcomes, and subgroup analyses prior to data access.
- [A Pre-Analysis Plan Checklist (David McKenzie, World Bank Development Impact)](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/impactevaluations/a-pre-analysis-plan-checklist) - A practical ten-point checklist covering statistical power, model specification, multiple inference corrections, and attrition protocols.
- [A Guide to Preregistration and Registered Reports (Open Science Framework)](https://doi.org/10.31222/osf.io/x7aqr) - A comprehensive guide to drafting and depositing time-stamped study registrations and registered reports to prevent publication bias and p-hacking.

## Run your own randomized evaluations

- [Test, Learn, Adapt: Developing Public Policy with Randomised Controlled Trials (UK Cabinet Office & Behavioural Insights Team)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/test-learn-adapt-developing-public-policy-with-randomised-controlled-trials) - A foundational, 9-step operational guide detailing how to design, randomize, implement, and evaluate field interventions.
- [Checklist for Launching a Randomized Evaluation in the United States (J-PAL North America)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/checklist-launching-randomized-evaluation-united-states) - An actionable project management checklist covering administrative steps, randomization strategies, institutional review protocols, and data infrastructure.
- [Introduction to Randomized Evaluations (J-PAL)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/introduction-randomized-evaluations) - An intuitive, methodologically rigorous walkthrough covering counterfactual reasoning, randomization units, and common evaluation pitfalls.

## Write your own behavioral policy briefs

- [Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab)](https://www.capolicylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Policy-Brief-Guide.pdf) - A targeted, 6-page manual demonstrating how to structure executive summaries, visualize behavioral data, and formulate actionable policy recommendations.
- [How to Write a Policy Brief (International Development Research Centre)](https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/funding/resources-idrc-grantees/how-write-policy-brief) - A modular guide and template for organizing research findings into concise, decision-focused briefs for policymakers.
- [Writing Briefs (CDC POLARIS)](https://www.cdc.gov/polaris/php/policy-resources/writing-briefs.html) - A structured public policy framework categorizing four types of briefing documents and the sequential steps required to inform government stakeholder decisions.
