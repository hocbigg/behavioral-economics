# Advanced Topics

Learners are encouraged to select one or two tracks aligned with their professional or academic goals rather than attempting to complete every specialization.

- **Behavioral Finance** - The study of psychological biases in asset pricing, market anomalies, speculative bubbles, and retail and institutional investment behavior.
- **Behavioral Public Policy and Governance** - The application of behavioral insights to regulatory design, public health, taxation, social welfare programs, and the ethics of state-directed choice architecture.
- **Behavioral Game Theory and Experimental Economics** - The formal modeling of strategic interactions among boundedly rational players alongside laboratory and field experimental design.
- **Applied Behavioral Design and Organizations** - The operational deployment of behavioral science in commercial product strategy, digital UX design, consumer choice, and organizational de-biasing.

## Behavioral Finance

The study of psychological biases in asset pricing, market anomalies, speculative bubbles, and retail and institutional investment behavior.

Market Psychology and Systemic Risk: [ECON 252: Financial Markets (Robert Shiller, Open Yale Courses)](https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-252-11) - Nobel laureate Robert Shiller's complete undergraduate lecture series, featuring dedicated sessions on behavioral asset pricing, human foibles, speculative bubbles, and financial market regulation.

Behavioral Asset Pricing and Limits to Arbitrage: [Inefficient Markets: An Introduction to Behavioral Finance (Andrei Shleifer)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780198292272) - The seminal academic monograph formalizing noise trader risk, investor sentiment, and why rational arbitrageurs fail to eliminate asset mispricing in real-world markets.

Asset Bubbles and Speculative Dynamics: [Irrational Exuberance (Robert J. Shiller)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691173122) - Shiller's definitive investigation into the structural, cultural, and psychological mechanisms that drive speculative bubbles across equity, housing, and bond markets.

Investor Biases and Wealth Management: [Behavioral Finance (Duke University / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/duke-behavioral-finance) - A structured course available in free audit mode that covers retail portfolio errors, overconfidence, mental accounting, and behavioral debiasing in wealth management.

## Behavioral Public Policy and Governance

The application of behavioral insights to regulatory design, public health, taxation, social welfare programs, and the ethics of state-directed choice architecture.

International Evidence and Policy Implementation: [Behavioural Insights and Public Policy: Lessons from Around the World (OECD)](https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264270480-en) - An open-access 404-page report surveying behavioral public policies across 23 countries, with in-depth case studies in taxation, healthcare, consumer protection, and regulatory compliance.

Intervention Toolkits and Heuristic Design: [The EAST Framework: Four Simple Ways to Apply Behavioural Insights (The Behavioural Insights Team)](https://www.bi.team/publications/east-four-simple-ways-to-apply-behavioural-insights) - The primary practitioner manual from the world's pioneer nudge unit, providing systematic guidelines to make policy-targeted behaviors Easy, Attractive, Social, and Timely.

Behavioral Public Policy Analysis: [Behavioral Economics and Public Policy (IIM Bangalore / edX)](https://www.edx.org/course/behavioral-economics-and-public-policy) - A free-to-audit intermediate course examining how behavioral economics addresses discrimination, affirmative action, public goods provision, and choice environments in government programs.

Ethics, Autonomy, and Sludge: [The Ethics of Influence: Government in the Age of Behavioral Science (Cass R. Sunstein)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781107140707) - The standard philosophical analysis of choice architecture, addressing paternalism, subliminal manipulation, institutional transparency, and administrative burden.

## Behavioral Game Theory and Experimental Economics

The formal modeling of strategic interactions among boundedly rational players alongside laboratory and field experimental design.

Strategic Interaction Foundations: [ECON 159: Game Theory (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses)](https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-159) - A complete 24-lecture university course covering backward induction, Nash equilibria, asymmetric information, and real-time classroom experiments exposing systematic departures from rationality.

Behavioral Game Theory and Strategic Anomalies: [Behavioral Game Theory: Experiments in Strategic Interaction (Colin F. Camerer)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691090399) - The definitive text unifying psychological realism and formal game theory, covering bargaining games, coordination problems, social preferences, and strategic learning models.

Randomized Evaluation in the Field: [Evaluating Social Programs (Esther Duflo, Abhijit Banerjee & Rachel Glennerster, MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-14-001-abdul-latif-jameel-poverty-action-lab-executive-training-evaluating-social-programs-2009-spring-2009/) - A complete MIT OCW course package from J-PAL covering the practical design, randomization mechanics, power calculations, and threat management required for rigorous field evaluations.

Field Experimental Methodology and Causal Inference: [Field Experiments: Design, Analysis, and Interpretation (Alan S. Gerber & Donald P. Green)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780393979954) - The comprehensive graduate-level guide to conducting randomized trials outside laboratory settings, detailing statistical inference, noncompliance corrections, and spillover analysis.

## Applied Behavioral Design and Organizations

The operational deployment of behavioral science in commercial product strategy, digital UX design, consumer choice, and organizational de-biasing.

The Execution and Implementation Pipeline: [The Last Mile: Creating Social and Economic Value from Behavioral Insights (Dilip Soman)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781442650435) - The definitive guide to closing the gap between behavioral theory and actual execution, detailing how to conduct behavioral audits, map decision points, and eliminate cognitive friction.

Behavioral Product Design and Digital UX: [Engaged: Designing for Behavior Change (Amy Bucher)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781933820422) - A comprehensive practitioner textbook applying self-determination theory and behavioral insights to software design, habit formation, and digital customer journeys.

Subconscious Attention and Consumer Choice: [An Introduction to Consumer Neuroscience & Neuromarketing (Copenhagen Business School / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/neuromarketing) - A free-to-audit online course exploring the neurobiological and psychological mechanisms of consumer attention, sensory marketing, and subconscious decision-making.

Organizational Choice Architecture: [What Works: Gender Equality by Design (Iris Bohnet)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780674089037) - A field-tested guide demonstrating how to replace ineffective corporate diversity training with behavioral systems design across hiring, evaluation, promotion, and institutional governance.
