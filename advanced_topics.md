## Track Index

- **Behavioral Finance** - The application of behavioral economics to investment, trading, and financial markets, covering market psychology, bubbles, and investor biases.
- **Behavioral Public Policy** - How governments use behavioral insights to design better policy, including nudge frameworks, choice architecture in regulation, and the ethics of influence.
- **Experimental Methods and Behavioral Game Theory** - The theoretical extension of game theory to boundedly rational players, and the experimental methods used to test behavioral hypotheses.
- **Applied Behavioral Science** - The practice of deploying behavioral insights in business, product design, and development contexts.

## Behavioral Finance

The application of behavioral economics to investment, trading, and financial markets, covering market psychology, bubbles, and investor biases.

Market Psychology and Behavioral Finance Theory: [ECON 252: Financial Markets (Robert Shiller, Open Yale Courses)](https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-252-11) - Nobel laureate Shiller's complete Yale course covering risk management and behavioral finance. Lectures 11 and 12 focus specifically on behavioral finance, covering prospect theory applications to markets, overconfidence, anchoring in trading, and the psychology of regulation.

Asset Bubbles and Speculative Markets: [Irrational Exuberance (Robert J. Shiller)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691173122) - The definitive book on market bubbles, covering structural, cultural, and psychological factors that drive speculative booms in stocks, real estate, and bonds. The third edition includes Shiller's Nobel lecture and updates through 2015.

Investor Behavior and Portfolio Decisions: [Behavioral Finance (Duke University, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/duke-behavioral-finance) - A structured Coursera course covering common financial decision-making errors and how to avoid them, available in free audit mode. Complements Shiller's lectures by focusing on individual investor behavior rather than market-level phenomena.

## Behavioral Public Policy

How governments use behavioral insights to design better policy, including nudge frameworks, choice architecture in regulation, and the ethics of influence.

Foundations and International Evidence Base: [Behavioural Insights and Public Policy: Lessons from Around the World (OECD, 2017)](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2017/03/behavioural-insights-and-public-policy_g1g7590e/9789264270480-en.pdf) - A comprehensive 408-page OECD report surveying how 23 countries have applied behavioral insights to policy, with detailed case studies in energy, health, taxation, financial literacy, and regulatory compliance. Freely downloadable as a PDF.

Practical Intervention Frameworks: [The EAST Framework (Behavioural Insights Team)](https://www.bi.team/publications/east-four-simple-ways-to-apply-behavioural-insights) - BIT's distilled framework for designing behavioral interventions: make the target behavior Easy, Attractive, Social, and Timely. Revised in 2024 to reflect a decade of applications. The most widely used practical toolkit in the field.

Ethics of Behavioral Influence: [The Ethics of Influence (Cass R. Sunstein)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781107140707) - A philosophical treatment of when and how government influence is ethically justified, written by one of the co-authors of Nudge. Covers manipulation, autonomy, and welfare in the context of choice architecture.

Case Studies and Evidence Base: [Behavioural Insights Team Publications](https://www.bi.team/insights/publications) - A freely browsable archive of reports and working papers from the world's first government behavioral insights team, documenting successful (and unsuccessful) nudge trials across dozens of policy domains.

## Experimental Methods and Behavioral Game Theory

The theoretical extension of game theory to boundedly rational players, and the experimental methods used to test behavioral hypotheses.

Behavioral Game Theory: [Behavioral Game Theory: Experiments in Strategic Interaction (Colin F. Camerer)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691090399) - The definitive text integrating psychological principles and experimental evidence into game theory, covering bargaining, coordination, signaling, reciprocity, and learning in games. No free equivalent matches its depth and authority.

Field Experimentation: [Field Experiments in Economics (David Reiley)](http://davidreiley.com) - Course materials and research papers on field experiments in economics, from one of the pioneers of the method. Covers design principles, implementation, and analysis of experiments conducted in real-world settings rather than laboratories.

Experimental Economics Reference: [The Handbook of Experimental Economics, Volume 2 (Kagel & Roth, eds.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691213255) - The authoritative survey of experimental economics research across macroeconomics, charitable giving, neuroeconomics, and other-regarding preferences. Use it as a reference to dip into rather than a book to read cover-to-cover.

## Applied Behavioral Science

The practice of deploying behavioral insights in business, product design, and development contexts.

Comprehensive Field Reference: [The Behavioral Economics Guide (Alain Samson, ed.)](https://www.behavioraleconomics.com/be-guide/the-behavioral-economics-guide-2025) - An annually updated, freely downloadable reference work covering behavioral economics theory, frameworks, and applications. The 2025 edition includes an introduction by John List and comprehensive coverage of practitioner methods.

Behavioral Science in Development: [Introduction to Behavioral Science in Public Policy (World Bank Institute)](https://courses.wbginstitute.org) - A free online course from the World Bank's Mind, Behavior, and Development (eMBeD) unit on applying behavioral science to development challenges. Search for "behavioral science" on the platform to locate the specific course.
